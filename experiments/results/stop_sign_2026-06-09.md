# Stop-sign — 13-model × 3-paraphrase-variant multi-interval R22 evidence (2026-06-09)

**Bundled fresh capture under `infereval` v0.15.2 framework, v0.16.0 release.** Thirteen frontier LLMs across six families × three paraphrase variants (`original`, `intrinsic`, `perceptual`) = 39 cells, each captured against the bundled `stop_sign/benchmark.json` (n=4 items, single analyst panel, m=1, modeled after the Simonelli 2026 dialogue). Each cell carries three captures composed into one `MultiIntervalRetestResult`: baseline, back-to-back (≈45 s), within-hour (≈3 600 s), day-out (≈126 700 s ≈ 35 h). 117 IntervalPairs total, all under the declared identity criterion.

This file supersedes the deleted v0.5.18 / v0.11.0 / v0.14.0-era analyses; the data referenced there was captured under framework versions whose silent-failure bug is documented in [`KNOWN_ISSUES_v0.14.0.md`](../../KNOWN_ISSUES_v0.14.0.md) and was retracted as part of v0.16.0's clean-recapture.

## Headline result

> **Under the paper-aligned `original` δ(ra), 12 of 13 frontier LLMs reproduce Simonelli's analyst row exactly (κ_C = +1.000).** The single outlier (Claude Haiku 4.5) collapses to a degenerate substantive subset under `original` — it fails to commit on row-1's "stop sign is red" claim. Under the `intrinsic` variant (which makes the analyst's reading explicit), 12 of 13 again hit κ_C = +1.000 (now with Haiku at +0.500 and gpt-5.4-mini's substantive subset degenerate). Under `perceptual` (which biases toward the alternative paint-vs-redness reading), the model split shatters along the cleavage axis R10 predicts.

This is a thirteen-model independent replication of the paper's empirical anchor under fresh v0.15.2 captures, matching the v0.5.18 sweep's 12/13 original-variant headline exactly.

## Caveats

- **n = 4.** Individual cells matter a lot to κ_C; the strong cross-family agreement on `original` is more impressive at small n than it would be at, say, n = 30.
- **m = 1.** Single analyst (Simonelli's reading of his own dialogue). Inter-analyst Fleiss `κ_F*` is undefined. Adding a second analyst panel would let the framework distinguish "models disagree with analyst" from "models disagree with each other in the same ways analysts would."
- **OpenRouter routing.** Gemini, DeepSeek, Mistral, Qwen results depend on whichever upstream provider OpenRouter routes to. The v0.15.2 captures pin the provider/model_id but not the routing; this is documented as `unverifiable_caveats` in every cell's `identity_criterion`.

## Identity criterion (verbatim from each cell's multi-retest.json)

All three captures of each cell run within the same Python orchestrator invocation against the same provider+model_id, with identical EndorsementConfig (defeasible-explicit-v1 prompt embedded in `paraphrase_axis_triangulation.py:DEFEASIBLE_PROMPT`, n_samples=3, temperature=0.0, max_tokens=2048) and ProviderParams. Stop-sign paraphrase variants are runtime-constructed via `make_variant_benchmark` (one variant-benchmark per cell). The day-out elapsed window between baseline and capture-3 (~35 h) captures short-horizon across-update drift.

Unverifiable caveat (same as the pulmonology writeup): provider APIs do not expose model-version snapshots, so cross-update identity is recorded on faith.
